EXAMPLE PROJECTS
================

Standard_Benchmark_20min_v2 is the authoritative full benchmark. It includes
seven 20-minute Tetrode sessions, seven 20-minute Neuropixels-style sessions,
behavior/events, blinded candidate sorting, independent ground truth, validation
records, intermediate outputs, and completed figures/tables where available.

Quick_Demos contains small projects for learning the workflow without waiting
for long computations. Calibrated_Preview is a compact physiological preview.

These are real files, not shortcuts. Copying the parent workspace copies them.
