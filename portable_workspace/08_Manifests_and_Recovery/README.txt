MANIFESTS AND RECOVERY
======================

Prepare_Portable_Workspace.ps1 rebinds project recording paths after the whole
workspace is copied to another drive or computer.

FILE_INVENTORY.csv lists files, sizes, and roles. SHA256_CRITICAL_FILES.txt
protects key executables, installers, source recovery artifacts, and manifests.
EXTERNAL_DATA_CATALOG.csv lists intentionally external research collections.
PORTABILITY_VALIDATION.txt records the last migration test.
