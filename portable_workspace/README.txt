NEUROEPHYS AI COMPLETE PORTABLE RESEARCH WORKSPACE
=================================================

This folder is the authoritative, transferable NeuroEphys AI workspace.
Copy the entire codex_neuroephys_ai folder to another Windows computer or drive.
Then double-click START_NEUROEPHYS_AI.cmd.

The package contains a runnable Full application, installer, full source history,
two 20-minute benchmark families, behavior/event files, ground truth, analysis
results, documentation, validation evidence, and recovery manifests.

Read 00_START_HERE\README.txt before the first use.

IMPORTANT DATA BOUNDARY
The 76.5 GB Wang Shufei real recording is preserved separately because it is a
large original research input, not a reusable example or application dependency. Its
location and integrity information are recorded under 08_Manifests_and_Recovery.

PORTABILITY PROMISE
Copying this entire folder is sufficient for the Full App, source development,
the Tetrode and Neuropixels 20-minute examples, and their saved results. It does
not silently depend on shortcuts or junctions. External research collections
must be copied separately only when those particular real recordings are needed.
